# Posters loop on their own; scrolling only moves the Sky and the Day clock

Each Poster plays its Motif as a short loop while it is on screen, and the page scrolls normally; scroll position drives only the Sky colour and the Day clock. We rejected pinning Posters and scrubbing their motion with the scroll (scroll-jacking): it feels more cinematic, but it fights how visitors, especially recruiters skimming on phones, expect a page to scroll, and it ties every Motif to scroll position.
