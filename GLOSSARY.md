# A Day in Type

Tung Nguyen's portfolio, told as one day in the language of iOS: swiping from the first Poster to the last moves the day from dawn to night, as the Weather app's sky changes with the hour.

## Language

**Poster**:
One part of the portfolio, holding one Topic: a Hero and, while it is open, a Detail.
_Avoid_: section, page, slide

**Hero**:
A Poster's one designed screen: the face of its Card in the Switcher, and the top of the Poster once it opens.
_Avoid_: header, banner, cover

**Detail**:
The modules below a Hero, built only while its Poster is open.
_Avoid_: body, content, article

**Card**:
A Poster in the Switcher: its Hero, scaled down. Opening a Card springs its Hero to the full screen and builds the Detail below it.
_Avoid_: tab, thumbnail, preview

**Switcher**:
The default view: the seven Cards in one row that the visitor swipes along, the focused one in the middle with its neighbours at the edges, as in the iOS app switcher.
_Avoid_: carousel, overview, home

**Motif**:
The one motion idea a Hero is built around, such as the name being written out or the Activity ring closing.
_Avoid_: effect, trick, animation

**Time slot**:
One of the seven moments of the day that a Poster stands for, in order: Dawn, Sunrise, Midday, Afternoon, Sunset, Dusk, Twilight. Time slots set the Sky and the Day clock but are never named on screen; visitors see only the Topic.
_Avoid_: mood, theme, chapter

**Midday**:
The Time slot at the peak of the day, between Sunrise and Afternoon.
_Avoid_: middle day, noon

**Sky**:
The soft gradient of a Time slot, with its sun and moon glows, in the style of the iOS Weather app. Every Sky has the same shape, so two Skies blend into each other mid-swipe.
_Avoid_: theme, background colour

**Ambient light**:
The Sky behind the Switcher, which follows the visitor through the day and blends between two Skies mid-swipe.
_Avoid_: backdrop, wallpaper

**Ink**:
The text colour: white, on every Sky.
_Avoid_: foreground

**Hand**:
The handwritten face (Caveat) for each Hero's display line, such as the name, the roles and "Let's talk.". Everything else is set in San Francisco.
_Avoid_: script font, cursive, display font

**Accent**:
The single highlight colour: sunlight yellow, the same on every Sky.
_Avoid_: brand colour, primary

**Frame**:
The Liquid Glass controls that float over the Switcher and an open Poster: the name (or the close button), the Day clock, the Poster count and the Timeline. It is the site's navigation.
_Avoid_: navbar, header

**Day clock**:
The time of day shown in the Frame, which advances from Dawn to Twilight as the visitor swipes along the Switcher.
_Avoid_: timer, local time

**Topic**:
The resume-style name for what a Poster holds (Profile, Objective, Experience, Playground, Skills, Education, Contact), shown in the Timeline and above the Hero's title.
_Avoid_: section name, title

**Timeline**:
The row of seven Topics in the Frame, one per Time slot; choosing one brings its Card to the middle of the Switcher, or swaps to its Poster when one is open.
_Avoid_: nav links, menu
