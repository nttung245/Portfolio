# A Day in Type

Tung Nguyen's portfolio, told as one day of kinetic-type posters: scrolling from top to bottom moves the day from dawn to twilight.

## Language

**Poster**:
One full-screen part of the portfolio, built around a single Motif.
_Avoid_: section, page, slide, card

**Motif**:
The one motion idea a Poster is built around.
_Avoid_: effect, trick, animation

**Time slot**:
One of the seven moments of the day that a Poster stands for, in order: Dawn, Sunrise, Midday, Afternoon, Sunset, Dusk, Twilight.
_Avoid_: mood, theme, chapter

**Midday**:
The Time slot at the peak of the day, between Sunrise and Afternoon.
_Avoid_: middle day, noon

**Sky**:
The flat background colour of a Time slot; between Posters it blends into the next Time slot's Sky.
_Avoid_: theme, background gradient

**Ink**:
The text colour of a Time slot.
_Avoid_: foreground

**Accent**:
The single highlight colour of a Time slot.
_Avoid_: brand colour, primary

**Frame**:
The fixed layer along the screen edges that carries the name, the Day clock and the Timeline; it is the site's navigation.
_Avoid_: navbar, header

**Day clock**:
The time of day shown in the Frame, which advances from Dawn to Twilight as the visitor scrolls.
_Avoid_: timer, local time

**Topic**:
The resume-style name for what a Poster holds (Profile, Objective, Experience, Playground, Skills, Education, Contact), shown in the Timeline and at the top of the Poster.
_Avoid_: section name, title

**Timeline**:
The row of seven Topics in the Frame, one per Time slot; choosing one jumps to its Poster.
_Avoid_: nav links, menu
